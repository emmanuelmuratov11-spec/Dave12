Privacy Policy

What we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains how Mindful Marketing Group ("we", "us" or "our") handles information collected through this website. By using the site, you agree to the practices described here.

Information you give us

  • Application form: your name, email, phone number, business name, website or social media handle, what you need help with, your budget range and your goals.
  • Contact form: your name, email, phone number (optional) and your message.
  • Booking a call: if you schedule a call through our booking tool, the details you enter there, such as your name, email and chosen time.
  • Materials you send us: for example, ads or other content you share so we can review them.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type and the pages you view. This is used to run and secure the site. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on this website.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your application or message.
  • To decide whether we're a good fit and to schedule and hold your strategy call.
  • To provide services you sign up for.
  • To follow up about your inquiry. If we ever send you marketing emails, you can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.

How we share it

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We only share it with service providers that help us run this website and our business, and only as needed for them to do that work:

  • Netlify, which hosts this website and receives form submissions.
  • Our scheduling tool (such as Calendly), if you book a call.
  • Google Fonts, which loads the fonts on this site. Your browser connects to Google's servers to download them.
  • Our email provider, which we use to reply to you.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

Cookies and third-party tools

This website does not set its own cookies. If you use an embedded booking calendar, that provider may set its own cookies under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, including any legal or record-keeping requirements, and then delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Depending on where you live, such as California, you may have additional privacy rights. To make a request, contact us. We will not treat you differently for making a request.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No website or method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This website is meant for businesses and adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the date at the top of this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Reach us through our contact page.
